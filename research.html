Research

I study integer programming and combinatorial optimization problems on graphs and networks. My work develops formulations, valid inequalities, and branch-and-cut methods, and tests them on applications where they matter.

Connectivity and political districting
Contiguity constraints, compactness objectives, and cut-edge minimization for building districts at the scale of real census data.
Graph partitioning and clustering
Low-diameter clusters, max k-cut, and connected partitions of planar graphs.
Optimization for quantum computing
QUBO reformulations and their penalty parameters, and classical preprocessing for quantum algorithms.
Applications
Healthcare operations, sports scheduling, interpretable machine learning, and supply chains.

Links to all of my work are on Google Scholar and GitHub.
