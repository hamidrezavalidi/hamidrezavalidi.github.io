Teaching

I teach operations research, integer programming, network optimization, and computing for engineers. In the classroom I lean on puzzles, interactive tools, and real data. Some of these tools have become papers with my students, including a classroom puzzle on kidney exchange and a scheduler for the FIFA World Cup 2026.

I received third place in the 2026 INFORMS JFIG Teaching Excellence Award and was named a Most Influential Faculty Member by the Whitacre College of Engineering in 2024 and 2026. As a graduate student, I received the Phoenix Award for Outstanding Graduate Teaching Assistant at Oklahoma State University.
